# Псалом 14 — промпт обложки

## Вариант A

Create a 16:9 music cover in a contemporary hand-painted gouache style. At
blue hour, show a close group of young friends from behind, seated with an
acoustic guitar on a stone terrace and quietly looking through an arch toward
a distant ancient hill city glowing in warm amber. Keep the group within the
middle 70% of the frame. Use indigo, charcoal, muted clay and restrained warm
light. No visible faces, text, logos, watermarks, crosses, halos, fantasy
effects or concert imagery. Avoid a generic wide landscape and dramatic rays.

## Вариант B

Create a 16:9 top-down editorial still-life music cover. On a dark rough wooden
table, show a round loaf torn into many irregular pieces and scattered crumbs;
one intact wedge rests inside a simple shelter-like fold of undyed linen, lit
by a narrow warm light. Use tactile realism, subtle film grain, deep readable
shadows and generous safe margins. No people, hands, text, logos, watermarks,
church props, blood, sky, grass or dramatic rays.

## Выбор

Выбран вариант **B**: разломанный хлеб и сохранённый кусок под льном.
