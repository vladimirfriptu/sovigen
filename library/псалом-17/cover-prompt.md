# Псалом 17 — варианты обложки

Три образа коллективной молитвы о защите: укрытие под крыльями, бережное
хранение и шаги среди угрозы. Финал оставляет надежду открытой: опасность ещё
не объявлена миновавшей.

## Вариант 1 — под крылом

Generate a music cover artwork in 16:9 widescreen aspect ratio, 1920×1080 pixels.

Mood / theme: a shared plea for shelter and protection, intimate and hopeful
but with a hint of danger still outside. Four young adults stand close together
inside a dim stone doorway, seen from behind at medium distance. Across the
wall and floor falls the broad, gentle shadow of a wing, suggesting shelter
without showing a bird or a supernatural figure. A little cool light enters
from outside; their faces are not visible.
Visual style: cinematic photography, natural textures, subdued indigo and warm
amber, contemporary and believable, soft balanced light.

Composition requirements:
- Keep the group and wing-shaped shadow centered.
- Leave generous safe margins: no important element within the outer ~8% of any edge.
- No text, no watermarks, no logos, no borders.
- Even, balanced lighting; avoid extreme detail in the far corners.

Output a single full-bleed image at 16:9, 1920×1080.
