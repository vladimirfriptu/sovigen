# Псалом 15 — промпт обложки

## Вариант A

Create a 16:9 music cover as a layered cut-paper and gouache illustration.
Frame the open entrance of a modest linen tent closely among dark stone. The
empty interior contains a simple woven mat and a small steady oil lamp, with
cool exterior twilight and warm interior light. Center the opening and keep
important details inside the middle 75% of the frame. No people, text, logos,
watermarks, religious decoration, fantasy temple, mountain panorama or
dramatic clouds.

## Вариант B

Create a 16:9 natural-light macro photograph of a weathered bronze tent peg
driven firmly into a fissure in solid dark rock. A natural white woven cord is
tied in a simple secure knot and pulled taut out of frame. Use documentary
realism, graphite rock, aged bronze and restrained morning light. Keep the
subject in the middle 70% with generous safe margins. No people, hands, text,
logos, watermarks, church imagery, stacked stones, flowers, sky or landscape.

## Выбор

Выбран вариант **A**: открытый шатёр с тёплым внутренним светом.
